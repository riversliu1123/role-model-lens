# Evidence handling

## Minimum record

Keep a concise evidence record for each used episode; this is an attribution trace, not a request for hidden chain-of-thought:

- Person and unambiguous source identity.
- Source URL or supplied artifact, title, publisher, publication date if known, and exact passage or timestamp locator when available.
- What was actually inspected: primary original, secondary reporting, user-provided excerpt, host/tool-provided excerpt, or inaccessible reference. Record the delivery origin separately from the publisher; a supplied packet is not direct page inspection.
- Event date separately from publication date, with the precision the source supports. Compute exact age only when birth date and event date are adequately established; otherwise use an approximate age, range, or life stage.
- Documented circumstances and action; distinguish contemporaneous evidence from later self-report. Promotional sources and memoirs are evidence of what was said, not independent proof of motives or causation.
- A relevant exact short quote, only if checked against the inspected text. Label a translation separately. If only secondary reporting is accessible, attribute the quote to that reporting rather than claiming original verification.
- Supported interpretation, material mismatch with the user's circumstances, and known counterevidence. Unknown considered alternatives stay unknown.

Do not label an entire record verified just because it has a URL. Inspection, attribution accuracy, source credibility, and applicability are distinct judgments. Do not turn heuristic retrieval scores into confidence percentages.

## Retrieval and rendering

Use decision conditions in search, not the user's identity or full private story. Respect available tool permissions, access restrictions, and source terms. Prefer original interviews, speeches, official statements, and contemporaneous reporting where appropriate; examine conflicting accounts rather than automatically trusting an official narrative.

Every biographical assertion must be supported by the inspected material. Connect advice to the relevant event and explain the transfer as an inference, not proof of what the person would decide today. Do not search only for confirmation of a preferred recommendation.

Prefer evidence that reveals actions, choices, constraints, or a working process over an inspirational quote that only shares the user's topic. Preserve a quotation's question and context when they affect its meaning. A self-described creative process is evidence of that account, not proof that it caused success or will work for this user. State what part of the user's question it can illuminate and what it cannot answer. Place the source next to the supported action or quote in the actual reply; keep the proposed application visibly your interpretation.

Keep quotes brief and necessary, follow the host's source-specific limits, and account for repeated excerpts from the same work. There is no fixed word count that establishes legal permission. Do not bundle lyrics, full articles, transcripts, portraits, or recordings by default.

When rendering speech in another language, label each quoted translation locally, including fragments reused later. Alternatively, paraphrase without quotation marks and identify it as a summary. Do not make quoting mandatory to satisfy this rule.

If source text is unavailable, omit the unsupported quote or claim. If no case matches well, say there is no sufficiently grounded role-model analogy and distinguish any general advice that follows. A quote is optional; truthful evidence is required for attribution.

## Distribution boundary

This skill ships a method, not a licensed celebrity archive. Sources retrieved during a conversation do not automatically become redistributable data. A license on the skill's original instructions does not license third-party names, likenesses, voices, trademarks, excerpts, or datasets. Historical status alone does not resolve source-material rights.

Public evidence-based discussion and endorsement or identity simulation are different product choices. Do not imply an official partnership, authorization, guaranteed likeness of thought, or legal clearance. Review actual deployment, branding, jurisdictions, and material use before public release; an open repository or disclaimer is not a substitute.
